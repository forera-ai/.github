# FORERA

### Engineering the Next Era

**Frontier Research in AI Infrastructure & Computational Efficiency**

Forera is an independent, research-driven engineering venture
investigating the computational foundations of artificial intelligence.

Our work focuses on understanding how intelligent systems execute,
where computational resources are consumed, and how alternative
architectures and techniques may improve efficiency, affordability,
and accessibility.

## Research Directions

- AI Inference Architecture
- Computational Efficiency
- Memory and Execution Systems
- Experimental Research Infrastructure
- Resource-Aware Computing
- Intelligent Systems Orchestration

## Projects

### Cachalot

Experimental research into large language model execution,
computational behavior, and inference efficiency.

### Cachalot Lab

Research instrumentation, experimentation, and performance analysis.

### Crewshal

Open-source infrastructure for intelligent agent orchestration
and reusable computational workflows.

## Our Approach

We believe computational efficiency requires more than
incremental software optimization.

Our approach combines:

1. Measurement and instrumentation
2. Computational modeling
3. Controlled experimentation
4. Architectural investigation
5. Practical engineering
